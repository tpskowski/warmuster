## Unreleased

- Unit cards are now standard 63 x 88mm trading cards, nine per A4 page.
- The Giant Goes Wild Chart is split out of the Giants' running rules: shown
  as a numbered chart in unit details and printed as its own two-sided card.
- Long special rules continue on the card's back; short cards get a Warmuster back.
- Card sheets print double-sided: flip on the long edge and every back lines up.
- Card stats print in two columns — Range, Speed and Half pace on the right.
- Cards pick the largest readable font that fits (quarter-point steps), and a
  bundled font keeps the fit identical on every device.
- A printer calibration control nudges the card backs sideways for duplex.
